What does “Google blocked” mean?

26 apps have observed service refusal in targeted later logs. Many still draw. The evidence does not establish how many apps fail their core task.

In the earlier 335-app l8f sample, 64 requested sign-in or location; 133 requested one of those or ads-related services. A single “15% blocked” claim cannot be reproduced without defining the API set and denominator.

Counting all non-allowlisted services gives 320/335, largely analytics and identifiers. Counting bundled SDK types is broader still. Neither count is an app-failure rate.

ConnectionResult 9 is SERVICE_INVALID. Its attribution to certification on this board relies on separate board/code evidence. Status 0 establishes a service connection, not a successful feature.
